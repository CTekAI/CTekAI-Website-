Sheet A-10 · Questions

The stuff people ask first.

How fast does it actually call back?

Within seconds of the lead coming in. Form fills, ad leads and missed calls all trigger a callback straight away, at any hour.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. It uses natural, human-sounding voices and talks like a good front-desk person. You pick the voice and the name, and you hear test calls before anything goes live.

What if a caller wants a real person?

It can transfer the call to you or your team, or take a message and text you the details instantly. You decide the rules, like always transferring emergencies.

What does it cost?

There's a one-off build fee and an optional monthly care plan that covers monitoring, tuning and support. You get a fixed quote on your call, based on your lead sources and tools. No long-term contract.

How long does setup take?

Usually 1–2 weeks from our first call to going live, depending on how many lead sources and tools we're connecting.

Do I need to change my phone number or CRM?

No. It works with your existing number and the tools you already use. If you don't have a CRM, bookings can go straight to your calendar and phone.

What happens after it's live?

We keep an eye on it. We review calls, fix anything that's off and send you a monthly report on leads, callbacks and bookings. When you're ready, we add the next automation on top.
