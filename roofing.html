Sheet R-08 · Questions

What roofers ask first.

Can it handle a storm's worth of leads at once?

Yes. It doesn't queue calls like a person does. Every lead gets called back within seconds, whether one comes in or fifty.

Can it talk about insurance claims?

It asks whether a claim is open and notes the details for your rep. It won't give advice on coverage. We set exactly what it should and shouldn't say together.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. It uses natural, human-sounding voices and talks like a good office manager. You pick the voice and hear test calls before anything goes live.

What if a homeowner wants a real person?

It can transfer the call to you or a rep, or take a message and text you the details instantly. Active leaks can always go straight to a person.

Does it work with JobNimbus, AccuLynx or Roofr?

Yes. Leads, notes and bookings can go straight into your CRM. If you're on something else, ask on the call. We can usually connect it.

What does it cost?

A one-off build fee and an optional monthly care plan for monitoring, tuning and support. You get a fixed quote on your call. No long-term contract.

How long does setup take?

Usually 1–2 weeks from our first call to live. Ideally before your next storm.
